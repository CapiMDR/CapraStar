About

This sketch is a chess interface powered by p5.js for the CapraChess engine.

Play local games with 2 players or against the CapraStar AI with an estimated 1900+ elo.

Challenge it on Lichess!

*If the link doesn't work, try copying and pasting it into your browser: https://lichess.org/@/CapraStar


Getting Started

Press the ▶ button to begin a new game from any board position.

Move pieces by clicking and dragging them on the board. All legal moves and rules are validated automatically.

  • draw
    Annotation – Right-click and drag on the board to add annotation arrows and circles.
  • navigation
    Game navigation – Click on the moves in the list to navigate through the game.
  • move_selection_right
    Premoves – Click and drag to make premoves while waiting on the AI. Right click to clear the queue.
  • bar_chart_4_bars
    Analysis – After the game you can navigate through alternate lines of play for analysis.
Controls
  • lightbulb
    Hint – Toggles CapraStar analysis.
  • skip_previous
    Undo All – Reverts to the first position of the game.
  • fast_rewind
    Undo Move – Reverts the most recent move played.
  • fast_forward
    Redo Move – Restores a move that was previously undone.
  • skip_next
    Redo All – Restores up to the last position of the game.
  • help
    Help – Toggles this menu.
  • play_arrow
    Restart Game – Restarts the board and state of the game.
  • screen_rotation_alt
    Flip Board – Rotates the board to view the position from the opposite side.
  • timer
    Time Control – Allows you to choose the time control for each timer separately.

    *Can only be changed before the game is started.

Position Tools
  • Get FEN – Copies the current board position in Forsyth-Edwards Notation (FEN) to the clipboard.
  • Set FEN – Loads a custom position from the FEN string entered in the text field.

    *Must be used before the game is started.

  • Get PGN – Exports the moves currently in the moves list to the clipboard in PGN format.
  • Set PGN – Imports a PGN-formatted game for review and analysis. Copy the PGN moves list into the text field before importing (e.g. 1. e4 e5 2. Nf3 Nc6).

    *This will end the game automatically.

AI Controls

Enable White AI or Black AI from each timer to allow CapraStar to play either side. Enable both options to watch the engine play against itself.

*Can only be changed before the game is started.
